## 研究问题

Can a compensation receipt alone certify reversal of the exact original effect on the current target incarnation after cancellation or termination

- Independent publishers Temporal and Kubernetes support distinct lifecycle and ownership boundaries, neither proves a real compensation incident, actual sink rollback or this synthetic model
