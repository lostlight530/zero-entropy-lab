## 研究问题

Does an asynchronous Activity Task Token ACK, rejection, timeout or cancellation establish whether an external non-idempotent effect occurred, and can equal accepted receipt count prove the exact current task predicate after attempt or membership drift

## 来源依据

Accessed 2026-10-22, distinct publisher identities and distinct contract scopes
