## 来源依据

- [Amazon SQS standard queues](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSSimpleQueueService/latest/SQSDeveloperGuide/standard-queues.html), 2026-11-09 Asia/Shanghai 核验，standard queue 至少一次投递，可能重复和乱序

- [Google Pub/Sub exactly-once delivery](https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/exactly-once-delivery), 2026-11-09 核验，区域内 pull subscription ACK 与 redelivery 保证，不等于第三方业务 sink 原子提交
