## 研究问题

Can `States.DataLimitExceeded` redrive replay previously successful Map/Parallel members despite ordinary redrive preserving successes, and can member presence or receipt-count equality prove exact authorized external completion

## 来源依据

Verified 2026-10-21, two publisher identities with different claim scopes

- [AWS Step Functions redrive](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/step-functions/latest/dg/redrive-executions.html) — Standard Workflow ordinarily preserves successful steps, but `States.DataLimitExceeded` can rerun successful Map iterations and Parallel branches, with relevant retry counters reset
- [AWS RedriveExecution API](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/step-functions/latest/apireference/API_RedriveExecution.html) — redrive eligibility and same execution identity, same AWS publisher, not an independent witness
- [Google Cloud Pub/Sub exactly-once boundary](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/data-analytics/cloud-pub-sub-exactly-once-delivery-feature-is-now-ga/) — message delivery guarantees do not prove exactly-once external side effects, independent publisher but not AWS evidence
