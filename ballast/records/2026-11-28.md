## 研究摘要

This Daily isolates same-operation commit/abort finalization after timeout from choosing an opposite finalization, starting a new transaction after fencing, and reconciling a non-Kafka external effect

## 研究问题

Can a recovery agent safely switch commit to abort after a timeout, infer historical success from successful producer initialization, or retry a non-idempotent external effect when read_committed does not show an expected record
